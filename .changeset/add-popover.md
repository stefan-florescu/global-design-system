Add `Popover` (`Popover`, `PopoverHeader`, `PopoverTitle`, `PopoverBody`): click or hover trigger, twelve placements plus `auto`, offset and optional arrow, in the top layer. Click popovers are non-modal dialogs that take focus and return it on Escape; hover popovers also open on focus and stay open while hovered (WCAG 1.4.13).
