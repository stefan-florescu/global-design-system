Add `Indicator`, modelled on Flowbite's indicators: a dot, count or icon in `gray`, `dark`, `brand`, `success`, `danger` or `warning`, five sizes, a `bordered` buffer ring, `count` with `max`, a visually hidden `label`, and nine `placement`s that pin it to an edge or corner of a `relative` parent (`indicatorPlacementVariants` positions other elements the same way). Server-safe. `Avatar`'s status dot is now an `Indicator`; its API and look are unchanged.
