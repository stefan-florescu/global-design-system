Add the foundation tokens: 8 primitive colour scales (80 hex values) and 48 semantic colour tokens with light and dark themes, plus typography, a 4px spacing scale, border widths, radius, elevation, icon sizes, breakpoints and containers. The themes build exports `themes.json` and `contrast.json` and fails when a checked colour pairing misses WCAG 2.2 AA in any theme. Source DTCG files are exported as `@stefan-florescu/tokens/src/*`.
